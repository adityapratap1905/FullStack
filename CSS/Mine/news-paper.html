Education Reform Bill Clears Parliament

New policies aim to improve learning outcomes nationwide

In a significant development for the education sector, the Parliament today passed the long-awaited Education Reform Bill. The bill introduces several key changes, including curriculum updates, teacher training programs, and increased funding for public schools.

Education Minister Ananya Sharma said the reform is a “historic step toward building a stronger and more inclusive education system”.

City United secured their third consecutive Premier League title after a thrilling 2-1 victory in the final match of the season.

The Chief Minister inaugurated the new Technology Park yesterday, calling it “a milestone in our journey toward a digitally empowered future”.

Spread over 50 acres, the park will welcome more than 200 tech companies and start-ups.

It includes advanced infrastructure, research centers, co-working spaces, and amenities for employees.

Officials said the project will generate over 15,000 jobs in the next five years and position the city as a major hub for innovation and investment.

Several national and international companies have already set up their operations here.

The government also announced plans to expand the park in the coming years.
