Sticky Card Stack

Scroll down — each card locks in place as the next one slides over it.

Step 01

Plan

This card sticks near the top of the viewport once you scroll to it, and stays there while the next card approaches.

Step 02

Design

Because each card's sticky offset (top) is slightly larger than the last, they stack like a deck instead of overlapping perfectly.

Step 03

Build

position: sticky keeps a card in normal document flow until its scroll position hits the "top" value — then it freezes.

Step 04

Ship

Keep scrolling — this last card will sit on top of the whole stack.
